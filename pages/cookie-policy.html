WHAT ARE COOKIES

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help improve user experience by remembering your preferences and enabling certain website functionalities.

HOW WE USE COOKIES

We use cookies for various purposes, including:

  • To ensure the website functions properly,
  • To remember your preferences and settings,
  • To analyze website traffic and user behavior,
  • To improve our services and user experience.
TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

The types of cookies we may use include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality and security.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember user preferences to enhance functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used to collect anonymous statistical data.

MANAGING COOKIES
